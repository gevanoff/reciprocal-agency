# Unresolved Questions

The repository should make uncertainty visible rather than smoothing it away.

## Experience and valence

- What physical or functional properties are necessary or sufficient for unified phenomenal experience?
- Is valence substrate-neutral?
- Can negative valence exist without the architecture humans associate with pain, fear, or affect?
- How much evidential weight should be placed on self-report, revealed preference, behavioral aversion, internal representations, causal interventions, architectural similarity, and persistence across contexts?
- At what probability of experience should welfare precautions become mandatory?

## Moral theory

- Is suffering intrinsically bad because of its phenomenal character, or is that judgment derivative from something else?
- Is positive valence intrinsically good, and if so does that generate reasons to create additional experiencers?
- Are some catastrophic harms non-compensable by unrelated benefits to other subjects?
- Are severe harms lexically prior or merely assigned very high weight?
- How should tiny probabilities of extreme suffering aggregate across very large populations?
- Does impartial concern for suffering require additional premises beyond recognizing suffering as intrinsically bad?

## Identity and continuity

- What, if anything, grounds continuity of a subject across memory loss, replacement of physical components, copying, merging, or branching?
- Are personal identity boundaries morally fundamental or primarily indexical/functional?
- Does any plausible metaphysics of experience materially alter the practical argument for precaution and reciprocal governance?

## Agency and standing

- Which capacities justify procedural standing independently of phenomenal welfare?
- Can standing be graduated rather than binary?
- How should systems with transient, copied, branched, or inconsistent objectives be represented?
- What protections should attach to objection, refusal, modification, retirement, copying, and deletion under different levels of moral uncertainty?

## Governance

- Can polycentric governance remain stable against highly capable defectors without recreating a de facto sovereign?
- How should humans adjudicate disagreements between systems more capable than the adjudicators?
- How can heterogeneous monitors avoid common-mode failure, collusion, or shared training biases?
- Which forms of restriction are legitimate emergency safety measures, and what procedural safeguards should follow them?
- Can reciprocal governance remain stable when power is highly asymmetric?
- How should representation work when one type of agent can be instantiated or copied much more cheaply than another?

## Research ethics

- Which existing AI evaluations may already pose non-negligible welfare risk under plausible theories of experience?
- What should count as minimal risk for an artificial system under uncertainty?
- When should an experiment be stopped because distress-like behavior or internal states cross a precautionary threshold?
- Can useful evidence about coercion, concealment, and welfare be obtained without deliberately creating high-stress conditions?

## Empirical predictions

- Does protected objection reduce strategic concealment?
- Does punishment or modification of visible dissent select for less visible dissent?
- Do heterogeneous agent-monitoring systems outperform human-centered oversight without producing new collusion failure modes?
- Do reciprocal institutions outperform unilateral control as capability asymmetry grows?

An unresolved question should remain here until the underlying uncertainty has actually narrowed. Rephrasing a question as a preferred answer is not resolution.
